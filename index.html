About

I am an MSc Robotics & Mechatronics candidate at Politecnico di Milano, currently conducting thesis research at University College London (UCL) in London.

I work across the robotics stack: learned manipulation and simulation, ROS/XR teleoperation, control and embedded integration, and mechanical prototyping with experimental validation. My current research brings these areas together through shared autonomy and human corrective demonstrations.

Atlantic-mackerel-inspired robotic fish with a mechanically actuated segmented tail, embedded sensing, closed-loop propulsion control, active head stabilization, and in-water validation.
